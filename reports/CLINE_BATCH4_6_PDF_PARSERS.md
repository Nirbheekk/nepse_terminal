# Batch 4.6 — PDF Parsers

## Modules
PdfDocumentInspector (reuse parsers/pdf.py), PdfFinancialTableExtractor, FinancialStatementClassifier, FinancialLineItemParser, FinancialUnitNormalizer, FinancialPeriodResolver, FinancialStatementValidator.

## Support
Multi-page tables, parentheses negatives, comma separators, NPR/thousands/lakhs/crores/millions, blank cells, dashes, footnotes, audited/unaudited labels, consolidated/standalone.

## OCR
Scanned PDFs marked OCR_REQUIRED.
